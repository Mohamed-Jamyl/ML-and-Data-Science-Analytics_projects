1) Overall Financial Performance

The company achieved strong results with total revenue of $12.23 million and a gross profit of $3.36 million, representing a gross profit margin of 27.47%.

- Total orders: 700,500.

- Total units sold: 920,200 units.

- Average order value (AOV): $17.45.



2) Category and Product Analysis

The Toys category leads in revenue, while Electronics leads in profitability:

- Highest Revenue: Toys category with $4.32 million.

- Highest Profit Margin: Electronics at 44.93%, despite having the fewest units sold (115,300 units).

- Top Selling (Units): Arts & Crafts with 269,900 units.



3) Location and Branch Analysis

Downtown stores are the primary driver of sales:

- Downtown stores account for 55.9% of total revenue.

- Commercial stores follow at 23.2%.

- In terms of store age, established stores contribute the most to revenue at 42.1%.



4) Seasonal and Timeframe Performance

- Peak Season: Spring is the strongest season, accounting for 36.5% of revenue.

- Peak Months: April, May, and March saw the highest revenue levels (between $1.4 million and $1.5 million per month).

- Weekends: The majority of revenue is generated outside of weekends (over $8 million).




Recommendations:

1) Profitability Recommendation:

Given that the electronics sector achieves the highest profit margin (44.9%) despite low sales, we recommend allocating a targeted marketing campaign to raise awareness of this segment and increase overall revenue.

2) Location Recommendation:

Since downtown branches account for 55.9% of profits, the possibility of expanding in these areas or replicating their successful models in other commercial districts should be explored.

3) Seasonality Recommendation:

Given the significant drop in sales during August, we recommend launching back-to-school promotions or summer sales to stimulate demand during this sluggish month.
